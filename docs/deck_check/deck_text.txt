Opportunities

Refunds cover generation errors, not finished clips that are unusable.
Nothing sits before "pay".

Who pays today
Per-generation buyers. Runway TrustScore 1.1/5 from 340 reviews (Sept 2026). Adobe feature
request: "I lose too many credits doing trial and error" (219 votes).
Who would buy a pre-pay step
Studios and agencies running generation pipelines. Generator platforms as an embedded step.
Built by a Seoul producer who ran generative-video pipelines for 2.5 years. Tested by a teammate
in India.
No revenue or market-size figure is claimed.
List of features offered by the solution

Preflight: three rungs before you pay
Risk check (free)
Failure-risk estimate from 1,911 human votes, with the nearest historical prompts as evidence.

Gemini rewrite
Kept only if the estimated risk drops.

A still before a $3.20 Veo attempt
Veo 3.1, 8 s at $0.40/s (Google pricing page).
After generation: upload a clip for a Gemini second look. Its verdict is stored separately as
feedback.
