ABOUT THE SCORE

What this score is

An estimate from 1,911 human pairwise votes on 2024-era AI videos of how often prompts like yours received a vote rejecting both clips. Not a calibrated probability for today’s models (held-out AUC about 0.67). Machine feedback can make the displayed score a mixed heuristic proxy, while the original human votes remain separate.

The five-seed comparison: held-out AUC 0.67 vs target 0.68; cold-start IRT 0.71 vs baseline 0.70; strict train-only TF-IDF 0.72. The exact earlier 0.72 claim could not be verified.

ABOUT THE CLIP REVIEW

What the clip review is

Gemini gives a visual opinion on the clip and sampled frames, with timecodes you can inspect. Accuracy on known-defect clips is unmeasured, and one camera-movement flag was disputed during independent frame inspection. Use it as a second pair of eyes, not a final verdict.

Accuracy on known-defect clips is unmeasured (no confirmed-defect reference clip available). One owner clip was first inspected as a negative control. All nine owner shots are available and were independently screened, with 0 confirmed intrinsic defects in the sampled references.
